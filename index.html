01 / The atmosphere

More than a place to eat.

Night markets are lively public spaces where food stalls, shopping, games, and social life come together. The lights and sounds are part of the experience—not just the menu.

🏮

Lights · 燈光

Bright signs and lanterns create a welcoming evening atmosphere and help each stall stand out.

🍳

Aromas · 香味

Grilling, frying, steaming, and simmering make the market a sensory experience.

🗣️

Voices · 人聲

Vendors call out specials, customers order food, and friends meet to explore together.

🤝

Community & connection

Markets make it easy to share small dishes, discover local flavours, and spend time together.

04 / Two food cultures

Taiwan × India

Different culinary traditions, with a shared love of food as a way to connect. These are broad examples; food and customs vary within both places.

🏮

Taiwanese night-market culture

  • Many stalls focus on snacks and single dishes.
  • Night markets can combine food, shopping, games, and socialising.
  • Mandarin phrases can help visitors order and ask prices.
  • Examples include bubble tea, oyster omelettes, and fried chicken cutlets.
🪔

Indian street-food culture

  • Street-food traditions differ widely across regions.
  • Markets and food streets often offer chaats, snacks, sweets, and local specialities.
  • People may order in different regional languages as well as Hindi or English.
  • Examples include pani puri, dosa, samosa, and many regional dishes.
